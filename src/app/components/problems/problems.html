Core Challenges in Kenyan Agriculture

The Agricultural Crisis We're Addressing

Mzuri Organics was founded to tackle these seven interconnected challenges plaguing Kenyan agriculture

01
Critical Issue

Declining Soil Fertility & Overdependence on Synthetic Fertilizers

{{'70%+'}} Soil Depletion
{{'3x'}} Cost Increase

The Reality:

Kenyan soils are severely depleted of organic matter and beneficial microbes due to decades of intensive farming without replenishment.

Farmers rely heavily on costly synthetic fertilizers (CAN, Urea, DAP) with diminishing returns
Results in nutrient imbalance, acidification, and reduced yields
Almost no localized, science-guided blending that matches real soil needs
Our Solution:

PREFarm Precision Soil Management with customized organic-mineral blends and soil testing

02
Environmental

Massive Organic Waste Mismanagement

{{'50%+'}} Waste Burned
{{'3.5M tons'}} Annual Loss

The Reality:

Household, market, agro-processing, and farm waste is poorly managed across Kenya, representing lost economic value and environmental harm.

Valuable biomass is burned, dumped, or lost
Contributes to methane emissions, pests, and pollution
Farmers lack circular solutions to turn waste into value
Our Solution:

Circular Biofertilizer Production transforming waste into high-value regenerative fertilizers

03
Access Issue

Lack of Accessible, Affordable Regenerative Inputs

{{'80%'}} No Access
{{'2-3x'}} More Expensive

The Reality:

Smallholder farmers cannot access or afford quality regenerative inputs, forcing them into chemical dependency.

No access to microbe-rich fertilizers or soil tests
Available organic fertilizers are inconsistent and poor quality
No precision dosing tools or technical support
Our Solution:

Localized blending centers with affordable, consistent regenerative inputs

04
Economic

Farmer Poverty Cycles & Missing Income Streams

{{'$1.5/day'}} Average Income
{{'0%'}} Waste Income

The Reality:

Farmers remain trapped in poverty with limited income diversification opportunities and no value from waste streams.

Farmers earn nothing from their waste streams
Youth lack dignified income opportunities in circular bioeconomy
No guaranteed buy-back or value addition pathways
Our Solution:

Regen-Kilimo Circular Economy Model with guaranteed buy-back and income diversification

05
Knowledge Gap

Limited Knowledge on Regenerative Agriculture & ISFM

{{'85%'}} Untrained
{{'1:5000'}} Extension Ratio

The Reality:

Most farmers lack awareness and training in modern regenerative practices, perpetuating unsustainable methods.

Unaware of biological soil restoration and composting techniques
Training and extension services are fragmented
Need hands-on, community-led learning ecosystems
Our Solution:

Youth BSF & Vermicomposting Enterprises with practical training and mentoring

06
Health & Safety

Pesticide Dependence & Declining Food Safety

{{'40%'}} Overuse
{{'15+'}} Banned Chemicals

The Reality:

Chemical pesticide use is dangerously high and poorly regulated, creating health risks and environmental damage.

Chemical pesticide use dangerously high and poorly regulated
Soils lack biological resilience, forcing more spraying
Communities face serious health and food safety risks
Our Solution:

Microbe-rich biofertilizers that build soil resilience and reduce pesticide needs

07
Climate Crisis

Climate Vulnerability & Unproductive Agroecosystems

{{'60%'}} Crop Loss Risk
{{'-30%'}} Yield Trend

The Reality:

Degraded soils worsen climate impacts, creating vulnerable farming systems with declining productivity.

Degraded soils worsen drought and crop failure
Farmers lack climate-resilient models
No integration of waste reuse, biological fertilizers, and water-efficient systems
Our Solution:

Complete circular models that build climate resilience through soil restoration
