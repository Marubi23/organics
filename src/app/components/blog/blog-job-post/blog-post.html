Program Overview

Mzuri Organics (Gare Holdings Limited) is implementing the FungaFrass Bio (FF-Bio) sub-project under FS4Africa Open Call 1, funded by the European Union. FF-Bio is a multi-county demonstration and field evaluation initiative focused on scaling fungus-rich biofertilizer systems for safer, more resilient informal vegetable value chains in Western Kenya.

The project will establish participatory demonstration gardens across the five target counties and will generate field evidence on bio-based soil health approaches, pesticide-use reduction, crop performance, soil health, farmer economics, adoption, residue compliance and food safety outcomes.

Target Counties: Kakamega, Bungoma, Siaya, Uasin Gishu, Vihiga
Funding Source: European Union • FS4Africa
Application Deadline: 25 June 2026, 5:00 PM EAT

Purpose of the Opportunity

Mzuri Organics seeks to engage one Graduate Research Assistant on a part-time, project-based basis to support field research, monitoring, data management, sample tracking, farmer engagement, evidence generation, and research dissemination under the FF-Bio project.

A key final ambition of the project is to develop a science-backed manuscript for submission to a peer-reviewed scientific journal. The Graduate Research Assistant will support literature review, evidence synthesis, preparation of tables and figures, and manuscript drafting under the guidance of the project's technical team.

Key Responsibilities

Field Research & Site Support

  • Support implementation and monitoring of FF-Bio field research activities
  • Assist with field visits, farmer engagement, training sessions, and field days
  • Support verification of plot layout, treatment allocation, and crop management records

Data Collection & Quality

  • Assist with baseline, midline, and endline data collection
  • Carry out routine data checks and support data cleaning
  • Maintain organised digital and physical evidence folders

Sampling Support

  • Support soil and crop sample planning and labelling
  • Ensure sampling records capture required metadata
  • Monitor agronomic indicators and produce quality

Scientific Publication

  • Conduct targeted literature reviews
  • Assist in organising and interpreting field datasets
  • Contribute to manuscript preparation for peer-reviewed journals

Required Qualifications

Essential:

  • Ongoing or completed Master's degree in Soil Science, Agronomy, Horticulture, Environmental Science, Plant Pathology, Agricultural Extension, Food Safety, Microbiology, or related field
  • Strong interest in regenerative agriculture, soil health, biofertilizers, IPM, and smallholder farming systems
  • Good understanding of research methods, data collection, literature review, and scientific reporting
  • Ability to work in field conditions across rural project sites

Added Advantage:

  • Prior experience in academic writing or scientific publication
  • Evidence of contribution to published papers or conference presentations
  • Experience in soil sampling, crop monitoring, or pesticide-use surveys
  • Understanding of soil biology or food safety testing
