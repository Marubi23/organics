Mzuri Organics Core Solutions

Bioconversion Initiative

Converting organic waste from Kenyan farms and markets into premium biofertilizers using Black Soldier Fly Larvae and vermicomposting technology

  • Sustainable organic waste management
  • Soil regeneration for Kenyan farms
  • High-protein animal feed production

PREFarm Initiative

Data-driven precision dosing technology optimizing fertilizer use for maximum efficiency and yield in East African conditions

  • Optimized farm input usage
  • Increased crop productivity
  • Environmental protection

Regen-Kilimo Program

Comprehensive training in regenerative agriculture and market access programs for sustainable livelihoods of Kenyan smallholder farmers

  • Farmer training & capacity building
  • Market access & off-taking
  • Community development initiatives

Contributing to Kenya's Sustainable Development

Mzuri Organics actively supports 7 UN Sustainable Development Goals through our regenerative agricultural practices in East Africa

1

No Poverty

Creating new income streams for farmers, youth, and women through buy-back models and waste-to-value enterprises

2

Zero Hunger

Boosting food security by regenerating soils, increasing yields, and improving crop nutrition through biological and organo-mineral fertilizers

3

Gender Equality

Empowering women in agriculture through training, leadership roles, and economic opportunities

4

Responsible Consumption & Production

Transforming organic waste into high-value fertilizers and creating circular, zero-waste farming systems

5

Climate Action

Reducing emissions, enhancing soil carbon, and building climate-resilient farms through regenerative agriculture

6

Life on Land

Restoring degraded soils, improving biodiversity, and strengthening ecosystem health with microbe-rich inputs
