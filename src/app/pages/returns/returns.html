Returns & Refunds Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Our Promise: 100% Satisfaction

We're confident you'll love our organic products. If you're not completely satisfied, we're here to help.

30-Day Return Policy

You have 30 calendar days to return an item from the date you received it. To be eligible for a return:

  • Product must be unused and in original packaging
  • Bio-fertilizers and feeds must be unopened
  • Proof of purchase is required
  • Perishable items may have different conditions

Refund Process

  1. Contact us at returns@mzuriorganics.co.ke with your order number
  2. We'll provide a return authorization and shipping instructions
  3. Pack the item securely in original packaging
  4. Ship the item back (customer covers return shipping unless item is defective)
  5. Once received and inspected, we'll process your refund within 5-7 business days

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund, including shipping costs.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened bags of poultry feed or pet food
  • Used fertilizers
  • Custom or bulk orders
  • Items purchased more than 30 days ago
