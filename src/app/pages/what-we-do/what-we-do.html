Mzuri Organics' Innovative Solutions

Transforming Kenyan agricultural challenges into sustainable opportunities

Bioconversion Initiative

Sustainable Waste Management & Soil Regeneration for Kenyan Farms

Leveraging Black Soldier Fly Larvae and red worms to convert organic waste from Kenyan farms and markets into nutrient-rich biofertilizers, regenerating East African soil health while producing high-quality insect protein for animal feed.

  • Organic waste conversion in Kenyan context
  • Soil nutrient regeneration for African soils
  • Insect protein production for local markets
  • Environmental cleanup in farming communities

PREFarm Initiative

Precision Dosing for Enhanced Kenyan Farm Productivity

Optimizing fertilizer use for Kenyan farmers through data-based precision dosing technology, ensuring crops receive exact nutrients while reducing costs and environmental impact in East African conditions.

  • Data-driven precision farming for Kenya
  • Reduced input costs for smallholders
  • Maximized crop yields in local conditions
  • Environmental protection in East Africa
